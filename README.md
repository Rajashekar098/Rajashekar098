### 📚 Project 2: MBA Research - AI Ethics & Organizational Impact
**Duration:** Jan 2026 – Jun 2026  
**Institution:** Frederick University  
**Research Supervisor:** Dr. Stavros Georgiades

**Research Title:**  
**"The Ethical Implications of AI-Driven Performance Management: Balancing Efficiency with Employee Well-being"**

**Research Scope:**
Explored how artificial intelligence and algorithmic performance management impact the psychological fabric of modern organizational teams, focusing on employee trust, psychological safety, and technostress.

**Key Focus Areas:**

🔹 **Employee Trust & Transparency**
- Impact of algorithmic "black-box" systems on team dynamics
- Role of transparency in algorithmic decision-making
- Trust erosion between employees and leadership

🔹 **Psychological Safety**
- Effects of continuous automated surveillance on employee creativity
- Impact on collaborative vulnerability and problem-solving
- Risk-taking behavior in metrics-driven environments

🔹 **Technostress & Cognitive Load**
- Physiological and mental impacts of constant monitoring
- Cognitive burden of algorithmic evaluation systems
- Employee well-being in data-intensive environments

**Key Insights & Recommendations:**
- ✅ AI has potential to remove cognitive bias and streamline data-backed evaluations
- ✅ Sustainable implementation requires **human-in-the-loop framework**
- ✅ Ethical accountability and clear communication are essential
- ✅ Employee well-being must be prioritized alongside efficiency gains
- ✅ Algorithmic transparency strengthens psychological safety and trust

**Research Methodology:**
- Quantitative Research Design
- Statistical Analysis & Hypothesis Testing
- Organizational Behavior Analysis
- Stakeholder Interview Framework

**Skills Developed:**
- 🧪 Quantitative Research Methodology
- 📊 Statistical Analysis & Data Interpretation
- 🏢 Organizational Behavior Analysis
- 💡 Critical Thinking in AI Ethics
- 📝 Academic Writing & Research Documentation
- 🎯 Data-Driven Decision Making
